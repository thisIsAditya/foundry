# Step 22 — IP, contracts, and privacy documents

**Phase:** 5  
**Sources:** IP India trademarks; contract basics; DPDP notices; starter notes (trademark, ToS, privacy).

## What this step is

Minimum viable **legal surface** for the MVBP:

- **Trademark:** search IP India before you print visiting cards; file when the name is real. Classes matter.
- **Copyright** in code/content exists without filing; still keep assignment from contractors.
- **Contracts:** founder agreement, contractor assignment, customer terms, refund policy.
- **Privacy / DPDP notice and consent** if you collect any personal data (you will: phones, emails, interview recordings).
- **Terms of service** that match the actual product, not a US SaaS template with California clauses only.

Patents are rare at idea stage; do not pretend you have one.
