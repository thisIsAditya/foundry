**Phase:** 4  
**Sources:** starter notes (skills, cost, speed — not fashion); PRD from step 13.

## What this step is

Choose tools for **this MVBP**, given the team and the time box.

Rules:

- The stack is a consequence of the PRD, not a personality.
- Prefer boring: Sheets, WhatsApp, Razorpay, a builder, Zoho Books.
- If you need custom software, pick what the builder can maintain at 2 a.m.
- India: GST invoices, UPI, vernacular, low-end Android if that is the user.
- Revisit after Gate C. Premature Kubernetes is a vanity metric.
