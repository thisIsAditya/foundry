# Step 18 — Team and skills

**Phase:** 4  
**Sources:** starter notes (MVP vs scale lists differ); Aulet company building later; YC cofounder advice.

## What this step is

Two lists:

**For MVBP:** the skills without which this specific MVBP cannot ship or sell. Often: founder domain access, one person who can implement the concierge or connector, ability to invoice, ability to talk to customers.

**For scale:** roles you must **not** hire yet (VP Sales before Gate C; big engineering before WTP).

Decide: solo, cofounder, contractor, agency, CA, fractional. In India, a good CA and a GST-aware bookkeeper often beat a second developer.

## Evidence required

- Skills mapped to steps 16–17 and 21–23.
- Hours/week the founder actually has.
- What will be hired vs agent-assisted vs ignored.
