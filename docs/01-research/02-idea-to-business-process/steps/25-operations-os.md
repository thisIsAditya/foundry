# Step 25 — Operations system

**Phase:** 6  
**Sources:** Blank Company Building (only after search); Zoho/Odoo reality; LivePlan plan-vs-actual; our product’s Phase C.

## What this step is

Install the **minimum OS** so the company can take money without chaos:

- Ledger / GST invoicing
- CRM of the actual beachhead (even a Sheet)
- Support channel (WhatsApp/email) with macros from real FAQs
- Compliance calendar (GST returns, DPDP, ROC)
- Cash and runway view
- Backup of the company pack (the take-away stays the source of truth)

Do not install a 40-app stack. Bind tools to the pack: pipeline stages named after the DMU; invoices that match `pricing.md`.

## Agent

Runtime ops agents — support, finance, compliance reminders — **approve-first** on money and outbound.

## Take-away

`ops_os.md` + live connectors (later).
