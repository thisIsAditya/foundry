# Step 21 — Entity and registration

**Phase:** 5 (parallel; trigger when you will take money, hire, or raise)  
**Sources:** MCA SPICe+; Companies Act 2013; practitioner 2026 guides. Detail: [../india/entity-and-spice.md](../india/entity-and-spice.md)

## What this step is

Pick a **structure** and assemble a filing packet. Do not let an LLM click “submit” on MCA.

### Common India choices

| Form                | When it fits                                                 |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Sole proprietorship | Simplest; owner = business; harder to raise or hire at scale |
| Partnership         | Two+ people; still personal liability                        |
| LLP                 | Limited liability; some startups; DPIIT-eligible             |
| OPC                 | One person, company form                                     |
| Private Limited     | Default for raising, ESOP, serious B2B; SPICe+               |

### SPICe+ (Pvt Ltd / OPC / Section 8)

MCA web form: Part A name, Part B incorporation. Linked AGILE-PRO-S: DIN, PAN, TAN, EPFO, ESIC, optional GSTIN, bank account request, some state PT and Delhi shops registration. DSC (Class 3) for directors. e-MOA / e-AOA. Typical ROC timeline often quoted **7–15 working days** if clean. Professional cost often **₹7,000–₹25,000** plus stamp duty (varies).

Post-incorporation (typical): current account, share certificates, first board meeting ~30 days, auditor, Udyam, DPIIT if eligible.
