# Step 11 — Willingness to pay

**Phase:** 2  
**Gate B lives here.**  
**Sources:** Aulet MVBP payment requirement; Maurya Demo–Sell–Build; starter notes (preorders, LOIs, real payment link). Pretotyping. Mixo-style smoke tests.

## What this step is

Separate **“they like the idea”** from **“they will give you money or a binding equivalent.”**

### Strong signals (best first)

1. Paid order, paid pilot, or paid subscription (even small).
2. Written LOI / purchase order / “invoice us.”
3. Refundable or non-refundable deposit.
4. A real checkout where a stranger enters a card/UPI and you can see conversion (smoke test).

### Weak signals (not Gate B)

- Survey “would you pay ₹X?”
- Waitlist emails from friends.
- “Let me know when you launch.”
- Likes, upvotes, Product Hunt.

### Medium signals (supporting)

- Mixo waitlist from **cold** traffic.
- Time invested (they did a 1-hour working session).
- Introductions to the economic buyer.

## How to run it (Demo–Sell–Build)

Show a demo, a concierge, a video, or a one-pager. Ask for the sale **before** you build the platform. If they will not buy the promise, they will not buy the code.

Blutrich’s **revenue gate:** 5 paying customers who are not friends/family (consumer: more). Use as a Phase-4 target; Gate B can be **1–3** strong signals to unlock building.

## Gate B

Pass: at least one **non-friend** monetary or contractual signal at the intended order of magnitude (not ₹1 theatre unless that is the real price).  
Fail: only compliments.
