# Step 02 — Beachhead customer

**Phase:** 1  
**Sources:** Aulet steps 1–3 (segmentation, beachhead, end-user profile). YC: do things that don’t scale. Starter notes said “not small businesses” — that instinct is correct.

## What this step is

Pick **one** narrow group you can actually find and talk to this month.

A usable beachhead is:

- Homogeneous in how they buy and what they expect (Aulet’s three tests: similar products, similar sales cycle, they talk to each other).
- Reachable without a national brand.
- In enough pain to take a meeting.
- Not “everyone with a smartphone.”

Examples of narrow: “ICSE school coordinators in Bengaluru who already pay for a fee-collection app” not “education.” “Independent interior designers in Mumbai who invoice via WhatsApp” not “creatives.”

## How to do it

1. Brainstorm 6–12 segments (Aulet Step 1). For a platform, segment **each side** separately.
2. For each segment, answer: money, access, reason to buy, can we deliver a whole product, competition, bowling-pin follow-on, founder fit.
3. Choose one beachhead. The cost of a wrong choice is weeks, not death — but **not choosing** is death.
4. Write an end-user profile (role, context, tools they already use, where they hang out).

## Evidence required

- Named segment with inclusion/exclusion rules.
- Where you will find 20 of them (WhatsApp groups, trade clusters, a specific job title on LinkedIn, a mandi, a Facebook group).
- If you cannot find them, the segment is too vague.

## Common failure modes

- “SMB in India.”
- Picking the largest segment because TAM looks good.
- Multi-sided marketplace targeting both sides equally on day 0.

## Agent

Persona / Beachhead Agent — proposes 6–12 cuts, scores access, refuses “SMB.”
