# Step 09 — Pricing framework

**Phase:** 2  
**Sources:** Aulet Step 16; quantified value proposition (Aulet 8); Hormozi value equation (use carefully).

## What this step is

Price from **value**, not from cost-plus or from “what ChatGPT said SaaS costs.”

1. Quantify as-is vs to-be (hours, rupees, risk).
2. Price as a fraction of that value so the buyer still gets a clear ROI (Aulet’s teaching example: customer keeps most of the value).
3. Respect **approval thresholds** (owner WhatsApp vs ₹50k PO vs board).
4. Start higher rather than lower; discount with a reason (early adopter, annual).
5. Keep list price and discount policy written down.

Hormozi’s `Value = (Dream outcome × Perceived likelihood) / (Time delay × Effort)` is a **copy and offer** tool, not a scientific price formula. Use it for packaging (bonuses, guarantees, speed), not for inventing ARPU.

## Evidence required

- Value math with `ASSUMPTION` tags.
- At least one real comparison price from alternatives.
- Packaging: what is in the first paid tier vs later.

## Common failure modes

- ₹99/month because “India price.”
- Enterprise pricing with a self-serve DMU.
- Hiding the price because you are afraid — that blocks WTP tests.
