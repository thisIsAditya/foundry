# Step 20 — Operating cost estimate

**Phase:** 4  
**Sources:** starter notes; Aulet COCA (related but not the same); default-alive thinking.

## What this step is

A **monthly burn to learn**, not a Series A budget.

Line items: founders’ subsistence, contractors, LLM/API, hosting, SaaS (email, analytics, Zoho), payment fees, GST, CA, ads (probably zero), compliance, travel to customers.

Output: months of runway at current cash; cost per experiment; default-alive test (revenue ≥ this burn?).
