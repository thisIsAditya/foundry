# India — entity choice and SPICe+

## Choosing a structure

|                           | Sole prop  | Partnership                   | LLP     | OPC         | Pvt Ltd |
| ------------------------- | ---------- | ----------------------------- | ------- | ----------- | ------- |
| Liability                 | Unlimited  | Unlimited                     | Limited | Limited     | Limited |
| DPIIT startup recognition | No         | Partnership yes if registered | Yes     | Yes         | Yes     |
| Raise / ESOP              | Poor       | Poor                          | Limited | Awkward     | Default |
| Compliance load           | Low        | Medium                        | Medium  | Medium-high | Highest |
| Typical first step        | Shop + PAN | Deed                          | MCA LLP | SPICe+      | SPICe+  |

For an innovation-driven company that might raise or hire, **Pvt Ltd** is the default. For a one-person services business that may never raise, sole prop + GST can be enough. Our chooser must ask about capital, partners, and liability — not always push Pvt Ltd.

## SPICe+ (MCA)

Official: SPICe+ is mandatory for new company names/incorporation (from 2020/2021 EODB design). Two parts:

- **Part A:** name reservation (can be separate). Automated checks vs existing names and trademarks; resemblance can kill the name (Companies Act s.4).
- **Part B:** incorporation, DIN (up to a limit of new directors in the form), PAN, TAN.

**AGILE-PRO-S (INC-35)** linked services commonly include: GSTIN (optional), EPFO, ESIC, Profession Tax in some states (Maharashtra, Karnataka, West Bengal cited on MCA EODB page), bank account opening request, Delhi shops & establishment for new companies there.

MCA EODB page (as of research): SPICe+ bundles services across MCA, Labour, Revenue, and some states. EPFO and ESIC registration are **mandatory** for companies incorporated through this route from the 2020 change; numbers are not issued separately the old way. Bank account application through the linked form is also mandatory for those companies.

Process in practice: Class 3 DSC → fill web forms → download PDFs → affix DSC → upload → ROC scrutiny → Certificate of Incorporation with **CIN**; PAN/TAN on the certificate.

## Immediately after CoI (typical practitioner list)

- Confirm bank account with the partner bank
- Share certificates + stamp duty
- First board meeting and auditor (ADT-1) within statutory windows (~30 days — confirm)
- Registered office proofs
- Udyam, DPIIT, GST if not taken in AGILE-PRO-S
