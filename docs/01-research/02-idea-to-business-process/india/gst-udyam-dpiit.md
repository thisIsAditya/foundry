# India — GST, Udyam, DPIIT

Confirm thresholds against CBIC / GST Council notifications before display. Figures below are the **widely cited 2026 practitioner picture**.

## GST registration

**Aggregate turnover** (PAN-India, includes exempt/export/inter-state) vs thresholds:

|                                 | Normal-category states | Special-category (as notified; some NE states keep lower bars) |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Goods only                      | ₹40 lakh               | often ₹20 lakh                                                 |
| Services, or goods+services mix | ₹20 lakh               | services often ₹10 lakh in states that kept it                 |

**Section 24 CGST** — register regardless of turnover for listed categories, including many **inter-state** supplies, **e-commerce** operators/sellers, casual/non-resident taxable persons, persons who must pay under RCM, etc.

Voluntary registration: allowed and often wise for B2B (ITC, credibility).

**Composition:** typically for eligible small taxpayers with prior-year turnover up to **₹1.5 crore** (confirm eligibility — services rules differ).

**QRMP:** quarterly return monthly payment for smaller registered persons (turnover cap commonly ₹5 crore — confirm).

**E-invoicing:** government has lowered the threshold over time; 2026 guides often cite **₹5 crore**. Confirm before telling a user they are exempt.

SPICe+ can request GSTIN at incorporation; it is **optional** there. Inter-state/e-commerce still forces the issue.

Returns: GSTR-1, GSTR-3B (monthly or QRMP), GSTR-9 if applicable. This is why ops Phase C needs a **compliance calendar**, not a chatbot reminder once.

## Udyam (MSME)

Free registration at udyamregistration.gov.in. Unlocks MSME benefits (priority lending narratives, TReDS access, some tender preferences). Do it after you have PAN and, typically, the entity.

## DPIIT Startup India recognition

Separate from incorporation. Practitioner 2026 eligibility (verify on startupindia.gov.in):

- Pvt Ltd, LLP, or registered partnership
- Age cap (commonly **10 years** from incorporation; biotech longer in some versions)
- Turnover cap (commonly **₹200 crore**; deep-tech variants discussed at ₹300 crore — confirm current notification)
- Innovation / scalability / wealth or job creation — not a generic trading shop
- 80-IAC tax holiday is **extra** (DPIIT + CBDT; incorporation deadline conditions have been used in the past, e.g. on/before 1 Apr 2030 in some explainers)

Benefits: easier public procurement, IPR fast-track narratives, some compliance relaxations, branding. Not a GST exemption.
