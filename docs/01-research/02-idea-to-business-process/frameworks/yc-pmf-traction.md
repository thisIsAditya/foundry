# YC, Sequoia, PMF, and Traction (practitioner layer)

Academic frameworks tell you how to search. Practitioner culture tells you what “good” looks like when talking to users, angels, and later-stage operators.

## Y Combinator (Startup School + essays)

Recurring rules:

- Make something people want.
- Talk to users constantly.
- Launch sooner than you are comfortable.
- Do things that don’t scale (manual onboarding, founder sales, WhatsApp support).
- Build a default-alive company if you can (revenue > costs) rather than a default-dead one that needs a round.
- A written plan is less important than rate of improvement and user love.

**Encode:** first-100 plan is founder-led and manual. Support macros come later. “Default alive” is a finance-agent view for bootstrappers in India (most of our users).

## Sequoia-style narrative

Sequoia’s famous “writing a business plan” memo (and later pitch culture) wants: problem, solution, why now, market, competition, product, business model, team, financials. The **why now** slide is the piece Aulet under-weights and we should add (regulation change, cost drop, new behavior).

**Encode:** a 10-slide view generated from the pack, including Why Now.

## Product-market fit tests

- **Sean Ellis:** survey existing users — “how would you feel if you could no longer use this?” **40%+ “very disappointed”** is the classic threshold. Useless before you have users who have used it.
- **Superhuman / Rahul Vohra:** segment that 40% by who they are; double down on the people who already love it (a beachhead inside your user base).
- **Retention curves** that flatten are better than a PMF slogan.

**Encode:** PMF survey agent only after Gate C. Do not put “PMF score” on a canvas generated from a paragraph.

## Traction (Weinberg & Mares)

Nineteen channels (viral, PR, SEM, SEO, content, email, engineering as marketing, targeting blogs, business development, sales, affiliate, existing platforms, trade shows, offline ads, SPE, community, engineering, etc. — the book’s list). The **bullseye**: brainstorm, then run cheap tests in parallel, then double down on the inner ring.

**Encode:** GTM agent proposes 3 cheap channel tests, not a 12-channel brand plan. Paid ads are usually the wrong first test for an unproven offer.

## Pretotyping (Alberto Savoia)

Test whether anyone would use it **before** you build it: fake door, data sheet, video, Confederate (human behind the curtain). Complements Mixo-style landing pages.

**Encode:** pretotype is a first-class experiment type in step 11 and 13.
