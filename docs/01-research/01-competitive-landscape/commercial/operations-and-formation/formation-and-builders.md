# Formation, education, and product builders (adjacent)

These are not incubators. Founders will still use them **beside** us. We should interoperate, not clone.

## Company formation

| Product                             | Geography     | Job                                 |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **MCA SPICe+**                      | India         | Actual incorporation. Not optional. |
| **Startup India / DPIIT**           | India         | Recognition after CIN exists        |
| **Stripe Atlas, Clerky, Firstbase** | US            | Delaware C-corp, bank, 83(b)        |
| **LegalZoom**                       | US-centric    | DIY legal forms                     |
| **VentureKit Formation**            | US LLC bundle | Plan + entity SKU                   |

**Steal:** a formation **checklist and packet** (docs, DSC, DIN, e-MOA/AOA, AGILE-PRO-S options).  
**Hard rule:** we do not file MCA forms as an unlicensed robot. Partner with CAs or generate a brief for a human filer.

## Education (often free, high quality)

- **YC Startup School** — free, best-in-class “how to start.” We will be compared to it on advice quality.
- **Steve Blank’s Udacity / I-Corps** — customer development drills.
- **MIT Disciplined Entrepreneurship** courses.
- **SCORE / SBA** (US), **NASSCOM / Startup India** learning (IN).

**Steal:** curriculum sequencing.  
**Avoid:** pretending a chatbot replaces a good free course. Our edge is **doing the work with tools and gates**, not lecturing.

## Product builders (they eat “MVP” )

Lovable, Bolt.new, v0, Replit Agent, Cursor, Windsurf, GitHub Copilot, OpenHands, Devin-class tools.

The Blutrich plugin already treats these as **paste targets** (prompt-engineer agent). That is the right relationship: we write the PRD and the builder prompt; they write the code.

**Steal:** one-feature PRD + copy-paste builder prompt + one-week deadline.  
**Avoid:** becoming a coding agent company.

## GTM tools they will also buy

Clay, Apollo, Instantly, Lemlist, Attio, HubSpot, WhatsApp Business, Razorpay, Cashfree.

Our GTM agent should produce **sequences and ICP lists**, then send the founder into these tools (or, later, connect APIs with approval).
