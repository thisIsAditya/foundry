# Foundra (foundra.ai)

**URL:** https://foundra.ai  
**Category:** AI co-founder for startup validation  
**Price:** $39/month after a 3-day free trial  
**Maturity:** Small commercial product, 2026. Direct competitor to our **Phase A**.

## What it is

Foundra’s public story: take a founder from “I think this could work” to “here’s why it will,” **before they build**. Three phases:

1. **Spark** — test the idea
2. **Validate** — customer and offer
3. **Build** — MVP scope and launch plan

Along the way: persistent AI conversation, **15 strategy cards** generated from the chat, a Kanban planner by phase, plus a content library (vendor: 90+ articles, glossary, 11 free tools).

They explicitly contrast themselves with Lean Canvas (“napkin vs architect”) and with LivePlan (“validate first, then maybe write a plan for a bank”).

## Strengths

- Correct **job-to-be-done**: stop building blind.
- Persistent project memory (the failure mode of ChatGPT for founders).
- Task planner that tells you what evidence to gather next — closer to a coach than a generator.
- Price is in the “serious hobby / first-time founder” band, below PrometAI, comparable to LivePlan Premium.

## Weaknesses

- Still **conversation-shaped**. We could not verify that it blocks progress without interview evidence.
- No take-away company pack of the depth we need (legal, unit economics engine, ops).
- No India stack.
- No operations runtime.
- Vendor comparison pages are self-authored. Treat feature lists as claims.

## What to steal

- Three-phase language is simple enough for first-time founders.
- Strategy cards as **first-class objects** (not buried in chat).
- Kanban of _evidence tasks_, not engineering tasks, in the early phases.

## What to avoid

- Stopping at “build plan.” If we stop there we are Foundra with more markdown.
- Content-library as the moat. Articles are cheap.
