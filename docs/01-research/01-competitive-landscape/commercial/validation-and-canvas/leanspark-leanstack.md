# LEANSTACK, Lean Canvas, and LEANSpark

**URLs:** https://leanstack.com · https://leanspark.leanfoundry.com · https://leanspark.ai/leancanvas  
**Originator:** Ash Maurya (Lean Canvas, _Running Lean_, _Scaling Lean_)  
**Category:** Methodology product, now with an AI co-founder  
**Credibility:** Highest in the validation layer. Lean Canvas is used by on the order of **1 million** entrepreneurs (vendor figure, widely repeated in accelerators).

## Doctrine (this is the product)

LEANSTACK’s public beliefs, which should inform our agent rules:

1. Love the problem, not the solution.
2. The business model is the product.
3. **Demo–Sell–Build**, not Build–Demo–Sell.
4. Traction is the goal; everything else is distraction.
5. Be brutal with ideas, faithful to yourself.
6. Speed of learning (+ building) is the unfair advantage.
7. Constraints are advantages.
8. Spend time on validation, not perfection.
9. Just start.

Lean Canvas (2010) rewrites Osterwalder’s Business Model Canvas for startups under uncertainty. It **replaces** Key Partners, Key Activities, Key Resources, and Customer Relationships with **Problem, Solution, Key Metrics, Unfair Advantage**.

Use **Lean Canvas while searching**; use **Business Model Canvas once the model is known**. Our product should generate both, at different gates.

## LEANSpark (AI)

Maurya’s AI co-founder:

- Describe the idea; it questions you and fills the canvas.
- **7-dimension stress test** (0–10): Clarity, Desirability, Viability, Feasibility, Defensibility, Timing, Mission.
- Tells you **which assumption to test first**.
- Tracks what has been tested vs still a guess; flags when evidence contradicts the canvas.
- 90-day validation cycles in **2-week sprints** (6 sprints).
- Can import a canvas from a landing page.
- Persistent memory of canvas, metrics, experiments, pivots.

Maurya states he used LEANSpark to validate LEANSpark itself (public figures: 307 customers, $115K revenue — founder-reported).

## LEANSTACK platform vs free template

A blank Lean Canvas is free (and everywhere: Canva, Miro, Google Docs). The paid platform is collaboration, tracking, and now AI. Foundra’s comparison pages quote Lean Stack around **$25+/month** or **$99+** depending on SKU — confirm on leanstack.com before citing a number in sales material.

## What to steal

- Demo–Sell–Build as a **hard agent rule**.
- Assumption objects with states: `guess | tested | invalidated | validated`.
- 7-dimension score as a critic rubric (we can map it to our GO/PIVOT/KILL).
- 90-day / 2-week experiment cadence for the roadmap agent.
- Unfair advantage is allowed to be **blank**. Maurya’s point: most startups do not have one yet; pretending is worse.

## What to avoid

- Treating a filled canvas as a validated business. Maurya himself built LEANSpark because static canvases freeze guesses.
- Ignoring BMC entirely. Partners, activities, and resources matter once you operate. That is our Phase C.
