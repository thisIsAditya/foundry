# PrometAI

**URL:** Product Hunt listing and vendor app (launched 29 April 2024; still marketed heavily in 2026)  
**Category:** AI business plan + valuation generator  
**Maturity:** Mid. Strong packaging; not a discovery system.  
**HQ / team (public):** Wilmington, DE entity; distributed team (Armenia / US / Germany / Colombia). CEO Narek Abgaryan. Vendor has claimed very large founder counts in social posts — treat as marketing.

## What it is

PrometAI generates investor-shaped documents fast: business plan, financial statements, scenario/stress tests, and a **valuation toolkit** (DCF, trading multiples, residual value, dividend model). Strategy add-ons: SWOT, Porter’s Five Forces, VRIO, PESTEL, Strategy Canvas. PDF and (higher tiers) PPT export. AI image generation for visuals.

Positioning: “planning as an experience,” not “plan as a service.” Target: early-stage SME founders who need a packet that _looks_ fundable.

## Strengths

- Only widely marketed tool in this set that treats **valuation methods** as first-class.
- Strategy frameworks beyond BMC.
- Speed: reviewers describe finishing a draft in one sitting.

## Weaknesses

- Price is **3–10×** LivePlan for a thinner operating system.
- No customer interviews, no evidence gates, no ops, no India law.
- Valuation of a pre-revenue idea using DCF is easy to misuse. A polished number can create false confidence.
- Some directory pages conflate PrometAI with unrelated “Discord community AI agent” products — brand collision.

## What to steal

- Optional **valuation appendix** for founders who will raise — clearly labeled as scenario math, not a fact.
- Strategy modules (Porter, PESTEL) as _lenses the critic agent can run_, not as auto-filled truth.

## What to avoid

- Selling speed of document generation as validation.
- Leading with DCF for idea-stage companies.
