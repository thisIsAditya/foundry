# Other commercial planners (document generators)

These products matter because they set **buyer expectations** (“a plan in 15 minutes”) that we will have to unteach.

## VentureKit

AI plans, light market research, financial forecasts. Free tier with limited full plans; Pro reported around **$8–19/mo** depending on annual vs monthly (sources disagree — confirm). **Formation plan ~$169/year** (US LLC/EIN/operating agreement style). That bundling of “plan + form the company” is the right _idea_ and the wrong _geography_ for India.

**Steal:** formation as a phase in the same product.  
**Avoid:** US-only legal packed in as if it were universal.

## Venture Planner (ventureplanner.ai)

Guided questionnaire → plan. Reviews describe thoughtful questions and clearer flow than raw ChatGPT. Pricing in 2026 roundups ~**$14–40/mo** or ~$29/mo; 30-day refund often advertised. Not a financial-system product.

**Steal:** question quality.  
**Avoid:** stopping at a narrative plan.

## Bizplan (Startups.com)

Older guided planner, often bundled with the Startups.com network. ~**$29/mo** in comparison tables. Weak AI story, weak research, weak forecast vs LivePlan. Still appears in “best of” lists by inertia.

## 15MinutePlan.AI

One-time fee (reported **$69** Starter / **$99** Professional). Questionnaire + AI → formatted document. No model, no research, no tracking. Fine for a school assignment; harmful if someone submits it to a sophisticated investor as “work.”

**Avoid:** one-shot pricing that implies the job is finished.

## ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini (not products in this category, but they are the real competitor)

Every planner’s FAQ now explains why a general model is not enough: unlinked financials, hallucinated TAM, no lender format. That is true. It is also true that a skilled founder with browsing and a spreadsheet still beats a bad SaaS template.

Our product must be better than **“Claude + a good prompt pack.”** The only durable advantages are: evidence gates, tools (search, filings checklists, models that reconcile), persistence, export, and India-specific law — not prose quality.
