# LivePlan (Palo Alto Software)

**URL:** https://www.liveplan.com  
**Category:** Business planning system with embedded AI  
**Maturity:** High. Longest-running commercial product in this space.  
**Closest job to us:** Take-away plan + forecast + later plan-vs-actual. **Not** idea incubation or full operations.

## What it is

LivePlan is the default “serious” business-plan product for US small businesses, SBA/loan packages, and advisors. The 2026 pitch is that generic chatbots cannot produce a **linked three-statement financial model** or sourced industry research, and LivePlan can.

Claimed install base: **over 1 million** small-business owners (vendor claim). Google reviews advertised at 4.8/5. 35-day money-back guarantee (not a free trial).

Parent: Palo Alto Software (also Bplans, the public sample-plan library).

## Product surface

- **Plan writer** with step-by-step instructions and **550+ sample plans**.
- **LivePlan Assistant:** Help Me Write, Rewrite, suggested forecast categories, file import so the model “learns” existing notes.
- **Idea Canvas** (new): AI idea validation suggestions.
- **Forecast:** P&L, balance sheet, cash flow — spreadsheet-grade, not chat-grade math.
- **Pitch presentation** generated from the plan (PDF / PowerPoint).
- **Investor/lender readiness review.**
- **Premium market research** with citable sources and industry benchmarks (vendor says 1,000+ industries).
- **Premium performance:** connect QuickBooks Online or Xero, compare actuals to forecast, 20+ trend reports, automated board-ish reports.

## What independent testers actually say

A 2026 bake-off that ran LivePlan, ChatGPT, Venture Planner, IdeaBuddy, Upmetrics, VentureKit, Grammarly, and 15MinutePlan found LivePlan strongest **when the job is a fundable, internally consistent plan**, not when the job is “pretty draft tonight.” That matches LivePlan’s own (biased) ranking, so treat the _winner_ claim as marketing, but the _structural_ point as real: linked financials beat generated prose.

## Strengths

- Financial model is a real model. Change an assumption, the statements move.
- Language a banker understands.
- Advisor / educator / SBA-partner SKUs — distribution into institutions.
- Plan-vs-actual is the only mainstream bridge from “plan” to “operate” in this category.

## Weaknesses relative to BusinessGenerator

- **No customer-discovery workflow.** Idea Canvas is still a form, not 15 interviews.
- **US/lender shaped.** GST, SPICe+, DPDP, Udyam are absent.
- **One-active-company** is hostile to people exploring several ideas.
- **Does not build or run the product.** No CRM, support, or India invoicing.
- AI is an accelerator of an old template, not an agentic company.

## What to steal

- Treat the forecast as a **constraint solver**, not as LLM arithmetic.
- Import the founder’s messy notes and keep them as grounding context.
- A **readiness review** that scores the pack before export.
- Sample plans as few-shot industry priors — with a label that they are examples, not evidence.

## What to avoid

- Leading with “business plan” in the headline.
- Letting AI write market size without sources (LivePlan at least claims source visibility on Premium; we must do this everywhere).
- Hostage data: export to Word/PDF/Excel must be first-class, as LivePlan does.
