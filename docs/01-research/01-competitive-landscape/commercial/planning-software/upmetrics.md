# Upmetrics

**URL:** https://upmetrics.co  
**Category:** Collaborative AI business planning  
**Maturity:** High. Primary LivePlan alternative in 2026 comparison articles.

## What it is

Upmetrics sells AI-assisted business plans, financial forecasts (advertised up to 10 years on higher tiers), pitch decks, and team workspaces. It is aimed at founders **and** consultants who produce many plans.

Typical 2026 public pricing (confirm on site; it moves):

- Entry around **$14/mo annual / ~$19 monthly** — 1 workspace, small team, ~3-year forecasts, pitch deck.
- Professional around **$37–49/mo** — more workspaces, longer forecasts, scenario tools.

QuickBooks and Xero integration is advertised earlier in the stack than LivePlan (LivePlan holds accounting sync for Premium).

## Strengths

- Multi-member collaboration without LivePlan’s “one active company” choke.
- Pitch deck + plan + forecast in one subscription at a lower entry price.
- Consultant-friendly (multiple client workspaces).

## Weaknesses

- Still a **document factory**. No interview ops, no India compliance journey, no company runtime.
- Independent commentary (including LivePlan’s own comparison — biased) says market research is thinner and less well sourced than LivePlan Premium.
- Pitch support is often an outline more than a designed narrative.

## What to steal

- Workspace model for **advisors mentoring many founders** (our B2B channel).
- Accounting sync **on the ops side**, not locked behind “write a plan first.”

## What to avoid

- Competing with them on “10-year forecast” as a feature headline. Early-stage 10-year models are theatre.
