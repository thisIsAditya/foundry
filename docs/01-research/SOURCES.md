# Sources and method

Research conducted 15 August 2026. Primary pages were fetched where possible; GitHub metadata via the public API; secondary reviews used only when the vendor page hid pricing or when an independent test existed.

## Method

1. Map the category: planning software, validation/canvas, AI co-founders, operations/ERP, formation/legal, builder platforms.
2. Open vendor sites for claims, pricing, and feature lists.
3. Search GitHub for agent systems that take an idea to a plan, PRD, or company.
4. Reconstruct the idea-to-company process from canonical frameworks, not from a single blog post.
5. Cross-check India legal steps against MCA SPICe+ documentation and DPDP Rules 2025 notices.
6. Derive agents from the process, not from competitor marketing names.

Vendor comparison blogs (including LivePlan’s own “best of” posts) are treated as **biased**. They are useful for feature checklists, not for ranking.

## Canonical process sources

- Steve Blank, _The Four Steps to the Epiphany_ (Customer Development). Stanford course handout PDF.
- Eric Ries, Lean Startup (Build–Measure–Learn; MVP as experiment).
- Bill Aulet, _Disciplined Entrepreneurship_ (24 steps, 2nd ed. 2024). MIT Sloan excerpt; Wiley TOC; step summaries cross-checked against the published step list.
- Alexander Osterwalder / Yves Pigneur, Business Model Canvas.
- Ash Maurya, Lean Canvas and _Running Lean_; LEANSTACK doctrine.
- Rob Fitzpatrick, _The Mom Test_.
- Y Combinator Startup School / Sequoia “Writing a Business Plan” practice (as commonly taught; not copied verbatim).
- Sean Ellis product-market-fit survey practice.
- Gabriel Weinberg, _Traction_ (channel testing).
- Marty Cagan, MVP questions (usability, value, feasibility, viability, ethics).
- Teresa Torres, Opportunity Solution Tree.

## India law (confirm before product copy)

- MCA SPICe+ FAQs and EODB feature page (name reservation, incorporation, AGILE-PRO-S linked services).
- CGST Act ss. 22 and 24 — GST registration thresholds and compulsory registration.
- Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India Code) and DPDP Rules, 2025 (notified 13 Nov 2025; phased enforcement through May 2027).
- DPIIT Startup India recognition criteria as described in 2026 practitioner guides (confirm on startupindia.gov.in).
- Udyam / MSME registration on udyamregistration.gov.in.

## What we did not treat as ground truth

- Any single “19-step” blog list, including the starter `research.md` notes.
- Vendor “#1” claims (Forbes badges, “1 million users”, “trusted by 100,000 founders”).
- AI co-founder landing pages that promise a company that “runs while you sleep” without published customer outcomes.
