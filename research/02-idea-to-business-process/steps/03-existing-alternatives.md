# Step 03 — Existing alternatives

**Phase:** 1  
**Sources:** Lean Canvas “existing alternatives,” Aulet competitive chart (later), Porter (substitutes). Starter notes correctly said: spreadsheets, hiring a person, and doing nothing are the real competition.

## What this step is

List what the beachhead **already does** when the problem hits. This is not a list of funded startups.

Categories to force:

1. **Do nothing / live with it**  
2. **Manual workaround** (Excel, WhatsApp, paper, memory)  
3. **Hire a human** (CA, assistant, agency)  
4. **Repurpose a general tool** (Google Forms, Tally, Zoho, Instagram DMs)  
5. **Dedicated competitor products** (named)  
6. **Informal market** (the neighbour’s nephew, jugaad)

For each: cost, time, who does it, what they hate, why they have not switched.

## Why before interviews?

You need this list to ask better interview questions (“walk me through how you did it last GST filing”). Interviews then correct the list. Do not freeze it from desktop research.

## Evidence required

- At least the workaround the founder themselves used, if they are in-segment.  
- Named products only with a URL or a shop-floor name.  
- “Do nothing” always present.

## Common failure modes

- Competitor slide with 8 logos and no substitutes.  
- Claiming there is no competitor. There is always doing nothing.
