# Step 17 — Distribution: the first 100

**Phase:** 4  
**Sources:** YC “do things that don’t scale”; Traction bullseye; Aulet 13 and 18 (sales process); starter notes (first 100 more important than budget).

## What this step is

A **named, manual path** to the first ~100 users or ~10–20 B2B accounts. Not a brand campaign.

1. List where the beachhead already pays attention (the step 02 hangouts).  
2. Founder-led outreach: introductions from interviewees, WhatsApp communities, trade clusters, cold but personal emails, standing in the mandi.  
3. Pick **three** cheap channel tests (Traction). Kill two.  
4. Write the sales steps: first touch → demo → DMU → invoice → onboarding (Aulet 13).  
5. Forbid paid ads until the offer converts from manual traffic (Blutrich growth trifecta: outreach before retargeting).

## Evidence required

- A list of 50 specific targets (names or firms), not “we will do SEO.”  
- Script that talks value, not features.  
- Expected conversion math that matches CAC napkin.

## Agent

GTM / First-100 Agent — target list design, sequences, channel tests. **Approve-first** before sending bulk email (spam + DPDP).
