# Step 08 — Business model

**Phase:** 2  
**Sources:** Aulet Step 15 (17 example models); Osterwalder revenue streams; Maurya “free is not a model.”

## What this step is

Decide **who pays, for what, how often, and through which flow of money**.

Common patterns (not exhaustive): one-time + maintenance; subscription/SaaS; usage; transaction cut; license; razor/blades; ads; data (careful — DPDP); freemium; marketplace take-rate; services/retainer; hybrid (software + CA services — very Indian).

Rules:

- Start from how the DMU already buys (annual CA fee, monthly recharge, per-shipment, per-student).  
- **Free is not a model.** Freemium needs an explicit conversion path.  
- Two-sided markets: name which side pays first. Subsidizing both sides is a charity.  
- Marketplace GMV ≠ your revenue.

## Evidence required

- One primary model, one rejected alternative, and why.  
- Who invoices whom (important for GST).  
- What “unit” you sell (seat, location, GSTIN, SKU, job).

## Common failure modes

- Copying US SaaS pricing for a market that buys annual maintenance from a local vendor.  
- Ads before audience.  
- Taking a cut of transactions you do not yet facilitate.
