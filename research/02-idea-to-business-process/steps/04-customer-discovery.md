# Step 04 — Customer discovery interviews

**Phase:** 1  
**Gate A lives here.**  
**Sources:** Blank Discovery, Fitzpatrick Mom Test, Aulet primary market research, I-Corps. Starter notes: 15–20 people before designing anything.

## What this step is

Conversations with **real prospective users** (not friends, not employees, not people who want to be nice) to answer:

- Does this pain exist in their actual week?  
- How do they solve it now? (feeds step 03)  
- How much does it cost them?  
- Who else is in the decision? (feeds step 05)  
- What have they paid for already?

You are **not** asking whether they like your idea. See [Mom Test](../frameworks/mom-test-and-discovery.md).

## How many

- **Minimum:** 12–15 in the chosen beachhead, or until stories repeat.  
- **Better for messy B2B:** 3 people × 3 sub-segments, then double down.  
- **Not a substitute:** 500 simulated AI users. That is entertainment.

Founders must attend. An agent may schedule, transcribe (with consent), and grade question quality.

## How to do it

1. Recruit from the “where they hang out” list in step 02. Offer a small thank-you if needed; do not bribe for praise.  
2. 20–30 minutes. Past behavior. Workarounds. Money already spent.  
3. Ask for 2 introductions at the end.  
4. Log quotes, not summaries.  
5. After 5 interviews, rewrite the problem statement (step 01). After 12, decide Gate A.

## Gate A (pain evidence)

Pass if:

- Multiple independent people describe the same pain in their own words.  
- They already spend time or money on a workaround.  
- You can find more of them.  
- Compliment-only interviews were discarded.

Fail if: only friends talked; pain is hypothetical; they would “maybe use it someday.”

## DPDP note

Names, phone numbers, recordings = personal data. Consent, purpose limitation, deletion on request. See [India DPDP](../india/dpdp-ip-compliance.md).
