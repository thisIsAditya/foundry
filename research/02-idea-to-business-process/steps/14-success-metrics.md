# Step 14 — Success metrics

**Phase:** 3  
**Sources:** Ries (actionable metrics); Lean Canvas key metrics; AARRR; Ellis PMF (later); starter notes (“what number says the MVP worked”).

## What this step is

Declare **before launch** what number, in what window, means proceed vs kill vs iterate.

Good early metrics:

- Paid conversions from the WTP test.  
- Weekly active use of the **core** action (not logins).  
- Time-to-value (first GST invoice, first booking).  
- Qualitative: they come back without you nudging.  
- Cash: contribution after payment fees.

Bad early metrics: downloads, followers, “AI queries run,” TAM, press.

Write a **kill line**: “If fewer than N of the Gate B customers use the core action twice in 14 days, we stop building features and reopen interviews.”

PMF surveys belong **after** you have users (step 26), not here.
