# Step 24 — Launch

**Phase:** 6  
**Sources:** YC launch; Aulet 23 “dogs eat the dog food”; Ries.

## What this step is

Put the MVBP in front of **beachhead customers who are not the team**, collect the step 14 metric, and collect payment.

Launch is not Product Hunt (optional, later). Launch is 10 specific people using it this week.

Observe: do they pay, do they return, do they refer, where they stall. Be honest. If they do not eat the dog food, do not scale (Blank).
