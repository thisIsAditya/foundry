# Step 01 — Problem clarity

**Phase:** 1 · Problem validation  
**Gate contribution:** You cannot pass Gate A with a vague problem.  
**Primary sources:** Maurya (love the problem), Blank (problem hypothesis), Aulet (compelling reason to buy), JTBD.

## What this step is

Write the problem as a **specific situation a specific person is already in**, not as a market slogan.

Bad: “SMEs struggle with productivity.”  
Better: “Owners of 8–20 person GST-registered wholesale shops in Pune spend Sunday nights copying Tally invoices into WhatsApp because their salesmen take orders on paper.”

A problem statement needs four parts:

1. **Who** (even if still a draft — step 02 will narrow it)  
2. **When / trigger** (what starts the pain)  
3. **What they do today** (the workaround — step 03 goes deeper)  
4. **Cost of the pain** (time, money, risk, emotion) **today**

If you cannot name a last-time-it-happened story, you do not have a problem yet. You have a theme.

## Why it comes first

Vague problems kill more companies than bad code. Teams build “platforms” for themes. Interviews then bounce because nobody’s week contains your theme.

## How to do it

1. Write 3 candidate problem statements in the founder’s words.  
2. For each, add one real anecdote (from the founder’s life or a single conversation).  
3. Score: frequency (daily / weekly / quarterly / rare), severity (annoying / expensive / existential), budget already spent.  
4. Kill candidates that are rare *and* cheap.  
5. Keep the problem in the customer’s language. If they say “my CA shouts at me every month,” do not rewrite it as “lack of ERP integration.”

## Evidence required

- At least one **concrete last-time story** (even if it is the founder’s).  
- A first-pass **cost** (hours or rupees). Mark `ASSUMPTION` until interviews confirm.

## Common failure modes

- Solution smuggling: “people need an AI app that…”  
- Celebrity problems: “the industry is inefficient.”  
- Mixing 4 problems into one sentence.

## Agent that owns this

Problem Clarity Agent — forces the four-part template, rejects slogans, does not generate TAM yet.

## Take-away artifact

`problem.md` with candidates, scores, and the chosen statement plus anecdote.
