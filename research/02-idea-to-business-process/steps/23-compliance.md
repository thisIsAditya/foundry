## GST (India)

Typical 2026 thresholds (confirm current notifications):

- Goods, normal states: **₹40 lakh** aggregate turnover  
- Services (and mixed): **₹20 lakh**  
- Lower in specified special-category states  
- **Mandatory from rupee one:** inter-state supplies (many cases), e-commerce sellers, certain RCM, etc.

B2B startups often **register voluntarily** to issue tax invoices and take ITC. Composition scheme exists for eligible small taxpayers (turnover cap commonly ₹1.5 crore — confirm). E-invoicing thresholds have been tightening (practitioner guides cite ₹5 crore — confirm).

## DPDP

You are a Data Fiduciary if you decide purpose/means of processing digital personal data. Notice, consent (free, specific, informed), withdrawal as easy as grant, security safeguards, breach process. Rules notified **13 Nov 2025**; Consent Manager provisions **~13 Nov 2026**; broad fiduciary obligations and penalties **~13 May 2027**. Penalties in the Act go up to **₹250 crore** for security failures. Build the notice/consent UX now; do not wait for 2027.

Children’s data has extra rules. Sector overlays: RBI (fintech), health, insurance.
