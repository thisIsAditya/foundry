# Step 15 — Roadmap and feedback loop

**Phase:** 3  
**Sources:** Lean 2-week cycles; Aulet 24 product plan (later); starter notes (weekly/biweekly early). RICE for after PMF.

## What this step is

A **learning cadence**, not a 12-month Gantt of features.

Early (pre-Gate C):

- Weekly or biweekly: what we believed, what we ran, what we learned, what changes in the pack.  
- Intake: interview notes, support chats, usage of the one core action.  
- Explicit “we will not add features this week” option.

Later (post-Gate C): RICE/ICE, beachhead completion vs follow-on (Aulet 24), still one owner.

## Evidence required

- Calendar of review rituals.  
- A single backlog that includes **evidence tasks**, not only engineering.

## Agent

Roadmap Agent — proposes the next experiment or the next feature, never both as a mashup without a reason.
