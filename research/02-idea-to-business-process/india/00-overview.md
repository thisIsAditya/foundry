# India — legal and formal setup (overview)

This pack is for founders operating primarily in India. It is **research for product design**, not legal advice. Confirm on MCA, GSTN, Startup India, and India Code before generating user-facing copy. A CA/CS should remain in the loop for filings.

## Sequence relative to the 26 steps

Legal is **not** step 1. Talking to customers does not require a CIN. Taking **GST invoices**, **investment**, **employees**, or **lots of personal data** does trigger this track.

Practical triggers:

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| You will invoice B2B or sell inter-state / on a marketplace | GST decision (often voluntary registration) |
| You will raise, issue ESOPs, or want limited liability | Pvt Ltd / LLP via SPICe+ |
| You hire | EPFO/ESIC, shops & establishment, offer letters |
| You collect phones/emails/recordings | DPDP notice + consent UX |
| Name will be public | Trademark search |

## What US tools get wrong

Stripe Atlas, Clerky, and LivePlan assume Delaware/SBA. Indian founders need DSC, MCA V3, GSTIN, e-invoicing, UPI, and DPDP — in Hindi/English plain language. That is a product moat.
