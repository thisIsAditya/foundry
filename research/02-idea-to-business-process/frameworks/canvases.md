# Canvases — BMC, Lean Canvas, Value Proposition Canvas

Canvases are **views**. They are not evidence. Our product should fill them from the company graph and stamp each cell `guess | tested | validated | invalid`.

## Business Model Canvas (Osterwalder & Pigneur, 2010)

Nine blocks for how an enterprise creates, delivers, and captures value:

1. Customer segments  
2. Value propositions  
3. Channels  
4. Customer relationships  
5. Revenue streams  
6. Key resources  
7. Key activities  
8. Key partners  
9. Cost structure  

**When to use:** after the model is no longer a wild guess — operating design, hiring, partnerships. Strategyzer is the official software.

**Failure mode:** filling Key Partners with “Google, AWS, influencers” as if that were a strategy.

## Lean Canvas (Ash Maurya, 2010)

Startup adaptation. Replaces partners / activities / resources / relationships with:

| Lean Canvas box | Question |
|---|---|
| Problem | Top 1–3 problems |
| Customer segments | Beachhead + early adopters |
| Unique value proposition | One sentence, specific |
| Solution | Top 3 features only |
| Channels | How they find you |
| Revenue streams | How you charge |
| Cost structure | Burn to learn |
| Key metrics | One or two numbers |
| Unfair advantage | What cannot be copied easily — **blank is allowed** |

**When to use:** day 0 through PMF search. LEANSpark is the living version.

## Value Proposition Canvas

Left: customer jobs, pains, gains.  
Right: products/services, pain relievers, gain creators.  
Fit is when the right side maps onto the left **with evidence**.

This is the right place to encode JTBD without a separate academic module.

## How BusinessGenerator should store this

Do not have the user type into nine boxes as the primary UX. Have them:

1. Answer interviews and WTP tests.  
2. See the canvas as a generated dashboard.  
3. Click a box to see the underlying quotes, sources, and assumption state.

That is the LEANSpark insight plus ModelBox’s unit-economics binding, which neither LivePlan nor PrometAI does.
