# Canvas generators — Strategyzer, Xtensio, ModelBox, SeedAngels

These products prove that **filling nine boxes is a solved, low-margin problem.** We should generate canvases as views over a richer model, not as the product.

## Strategyzer (Osterwalder)

Official home of the **Business Model Canvas** and **Value Proposition Canvas**. Team workspaces, testing cards, innovation programs. Pricing commonly **$25+/month** for software; corporate training is a separate (large) business.

**Steal:** VPC split — customer jobs / pains / gains vs pain relievers / gain creators / products.  
**Avoid:** Competing with Strategyzer on enterprise innovation workshops.

## Xtensio

AI fills all 9 BMC blocks from a paragraph. Live shareable folio, brand kit, personas, pitch, SWOT, competitive analysis in the same workspace. Positions against ChatGPT (unstructured), Miro (blank), Canva (static).

**Steal:** canvases as **live links**, not only PDFs.  
**Avoid:** beautiful boxes with unsourced content.

## ModelBox (usemodelbox.ai)

Developer-flavored Lean Canvas: guided prompts, **auto unit economics** (CAC, LTV, break-even, MRR), assumption tracker, version history, investor PDF. Free one canvas; **~$19/mo** for AI + unlimited.

**Steal:** canvas and unit economics as **one object**. This is the correct data model.  
**Avoid:** letting auto-math on invented CAC/LTV look like accounting.

## SeedAngels generator

Free, no signup, one-pass BMC, inline edit, print. Upsell into their fuller “investor-ready project” (market analysis, forecasts, deck). Privacy claim: idea processed by OpenAI for generation only, not stored.

**Steal:** frictionless first canvas as a hook.  
**Avoid:** one-pass generation as the whole product (they use it as a funnel, which is honest).
