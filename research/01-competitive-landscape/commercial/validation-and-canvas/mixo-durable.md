# Mixo and Durable — validate online vs run a local business

Two AI website products that founders confuse with “starting a company.” They occupy opposite ends of the same funnel.

## Mixo (mixo.io)

**Job:** Turn a sentence into a **hosted landing page** with waitlist, SEO, analytics, subscriber export.

**Pricing (2026 reviews):** permanent free tier (with Mixo branding); paid roughly **$9–39/month** for custom domain, multi-page, extra AI generation.

**Why it matters:** Mixo is the cheapest honest **interest test**. A waitlist is not willingness to pay, but it is better than a canvas. Fitzpatrick/Mom Test still applies: signups from friends are noise.

**Steal:** one-click landing + waitlist as an experiment type in the WTP agent.  
**Avoid:** calling a Mixo page an MVP of a complex B2B workflow.

## Durable (durable.co)

**Job:** All-in-one **local/small business** presence: AI website plus CRM, invoicing, reviews, booking, SEO blog, marketing automation. Pricing commonly from about **$15–25/month** depending on promo (confirm).

**Why it matters:** Durable is closer to **Phase C operations** for a plumber, consultant, or clinic than LivePlan is. It is not an incubator. Mixo vs Durable comparisons correctly say: Mixo = test the idea; Durable = furnished office for a simple business.

**Steal:** after validation, offer a “simple business OS” path (site, invoicing, CRM) for non-software ideas.  
**Avoid:** pretending a software startup can run on Durable.

## Together they show a fork in our product

Not every incubated idea is a SaaS. The take-away pack should branch:

- **Software / marketplace** → PRD, tech advisor, builder prompts, Stripe-like billing design.
- **Local / services / D2C** → Durable-like ops (GST invoices, WhatsApp, bookings) rather than a React app.
