# FounderOS (commercial) and WorkoAI

These are **AI operating systems for companies that already exist**. They compete with our Phase C, not with our incubator. Both are early; both use the same rhetorical move: “ChatGPT answers, we execute.”

## FounderOS — runfounderos.com

Not the same as the open-source `vinicius91carvalho/founder-os` markdown pack.

Pitch: role-based AI team (sales, marketing, ops, support) pre-wired to *your* workflows. Strategy is written, sequenced, and pushed to production; human approves external actions. Contrasts itself with ChatGPT, GPT wrappers, and GoHighLevel.

**Pricing (site, annual-billed figures shown):**

| Tier | About | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | ~$124–149/mo | 1 AI employee, lead capture, reviews, email support |
| Growth | ~$374/mo | 4 AI employees, automation + content engine |
| Scale | ~$1075/mo | “Full executive team,” multi-location, custom workflows, strategist |

Comparison they publish: one human ops hire ≈ $4,500/mo vs AI team from $149/mo. That is a sales argument, not a measured productivity study.

**Status:** “Built by a solo founder. Early access, opening soon.”

## WorkoAI — workoai.com

Pitch: pre-built department agents (Sales, Support, HR, Finance, Marketing, Legal, Engineering) with **BDI** (belief–desire–intention) reasoning and **Contract Net Protocol** handoffs. Pause for human approval on sensitive actions. Custom agent builder.

**Pricing:**

| Plan | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $0 | Free on-ramp |
| Growth | $99/mo | Mid-market AI workforce |
| Business | $299/mo | 8,000 tasks/mo, SSO, custom analytics (as advertised) |

They benchmark against Lindy (~$50–200/user), Relevance AI (~$29–349), n8n (~$24–60) — i.e. they see themselves in **automation**, not incubation.

## What this means for us

- If we say “we also run your business,” we will be compared to these plus Zoho.
- Their weakness: they do not know **how the company was designed**. They attach agents to tools, not to a validated offer and ICP.
- Our Phase C should **bind agents to the company pack** (who we serve, what we charge, what we refuse to automate).
- Do not copy “replace a $4,500 hire” language without evidence.

## What to steal

- Approval matrix by action type (email send, refund, deploy, file GST).
- Department roles for runtime (support, finance, growth) **after** incubation.
- Task/month metering so ops usage is predictable.
