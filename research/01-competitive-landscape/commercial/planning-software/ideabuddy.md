# IdeaBuddy

**URL:** https://ideabuddy.com  
**Category:** Ideation-to-plan workspace  
**Maturity:** High for early-stage founders; weaker as a funding or ops tool.  
**Closest job to us:** The **Guide** that walks a novice from spark to a filled plan.

## What it is

IdeaBuddy markets itself as all-in-one planning “from the initial spark to execution,” and Forbes has called it a leading ideation planning product (vendor badge). The product is a **sequential workspace**, not a chatbot:

1. **Canvas** — one-page model (their “Idea Plan”).
2. **Guide** — industry-aware steps; completing them fills the plan.
3. **Financial Plan** — P&L and cash flow; Excel export.
4. **Business Plan** — assembled automatically from the Guide.
5. **Validation** — a scored test of assumptions (not customer interviews).
6. **AI Buddy** — suggestions, templates, writing help in nine languages, revenue/expense estimates.

Collaboration, link sharing, PDF/Word/Excel export, mobile-friendly UI.

## Pricing (official page, August 2026)

Exact dollar amounts are not printed as static text on the pricing page (plans are interactive). Structure:

| Tier | What you get |
|---|---|
| **15-day free trial** | Canvas, Guide, Financial Plan, AI. No credit card. |
| **Free** | 1 idea, 1 collaborator, **10 AI requests**, Canvas/Guide/Financial Plan. No full Business Plan, Validation, or export. |
| **Dreamer** | Starting paid tier for a sketch-stage idea (1 idea slot). |
| **Founder** | Full features: Business Plan, Validation, export, collaborators. |
| **White Label** | Custom domain/brand for incubators. Quote. |
| **Enterprise / schools** | Extra accounts; nonprofit discount. |

Secondary 2026 reviews put paid seats roughly in the **$12–25/month** band (often cheaper annually, vendor claims “save up to 60%”). Confirm at checkout. 15-day money-back after purchase. AI credits refill each billing cycle; extra credits can be bought.

An “idea slot” is one project (canvas + guide + financials + plan). Slots can be reused by deleting old ideas.

## Strengths

- Best **onboarding psychology** in the planning category: novices are not thrown a 40-page outline.
- Plan is a **by-product of the Guide**, which is the right information architecture (single source).
- Cheap enough for first-time founders and incubators (white label).
- Templates per industry (coffee shop through tech startup).

## Weaknesses

- **Validation is a quiz**, not discovery. It cannot tell you if anyone will pay.
- Independent testers in 2026 called AI Buddy “tacked on”: copy-paste rather than in-place generation, usage limits.
- No interviews, no India legal, no operations, no product build.
- Financials are “easy,” not LivePlan-grade lender models.

## What to steal

- **Guide fills the pack.** The user should never write the business plan as a separate essay.
- Industry templates as *starting hypotheses*, immediately marked unvalidated.
- White-label path for incubators, colleges, and Startup India hubs — a distribution channel.

## What to avoid

- Calling a self-assessment “validation.”
- Capping the core loop on AI credits so tightly that the product feels unfinished (reviewer complaint).
