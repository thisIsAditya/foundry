# Gap analysis — where BusinessGenerator can win

## The crowded jobs (do not compete head-on)

| Job | Why it is a trap |
|---|---|
| AI writes a 30-page business plan | PrometAI, ChatGPT, 15MinutePlan, Upmetrics, LivePlan already own trust or speed. Race to the bottom on “plan in 10 minutes.” |
| AI fills a Lean Canvas | LEANSpark is built by the inventor. Xtensio/SeedAngels are free-ish. Filling boxes is not a business. |
| AI builds the website/app | Lovable, Bolt, v0, Replit, Cursor. Capital and model access we will not match. |
| Generic “AI employees” for SMBs | WorkoAI, Lindy, Relevance AI, GoHighLevel, FounderOS. Needs integrations, not incubation IP. |

## The empty jobs (our product)

### 1. Evidence-gated incubation

Almost every generator will produce a TAM, an ICP, and an MVP list from a paragraph. Almost none will **refuse to proceed** when the founder has not spoken to customers.

Steal these patterns:

- LaunchPad-v2 **provenance tiers**: founder-asserted < workflow-derived < externally-verified.
- Business Plan Harness **Evaluator that cannot be the Generator**.
- FounderOS **GO / PIVOT / KILL**.
- LEANSpark **assumption tracker + 90-day experiments**.
- Blutrich plugin **process guardian** (instant fail on fabricated research, 4-feature MVPs, placeholders).

### 2. Internally consistent take-away pack

Founders need to leave with files they own: not a hostage SaaS. The pack should be single-sourced (price appears once; the deck, model, and ToS all read it).

Nobody commercial does this as a **versioned company repository**. OSS FounderOS and the Harness Mode-5 16-deliverable set are the templates.

Recommended pack (export: git repo + PDF/ZIP):

- Problem evidence log (interview notes, quotes, sources)
- Persona + DMU
- Lean Canvas + Business Model Canvas (both; they answer different questions)
- Alternatives matrix (including “do nothing” and spreadsheets)
- TAM/SAM/SOM with method and `ASSUMPTION:` labels
- Offer + quantified value proposition
- Unit economics napkin (LTV, CAC, payback)
- MVP / MVBP spec + out-of-scope list
- 90-day experiment board
- GTM for first 100
- Cost stack
- India legal checklist (entity, GST, Udyam, DPIIT, DPDP, trademark)
- Ops playbooks (support, billing, compliance calendar)
- Pitch one-pager (generated last, never first)

### 3. India-native company formation and compliance in the same product

LivePlan is US/SBA-shaped. Stripe Atlas is Delaware-shaped. Indian founders currently stitch MCA + GSTN + ClearTax + a WhatsApp CA.

A credible India pack is a moat that US incumbents will not copy quickly:

- Entity chooser (sole prop / partnership / LLP / OPC / Pvt Ltd)
- SPICe+ document checklist (not auto-filing unless we become a CA platform)
- GST threshold logic (goods vs services, inter-state, e-commerce)
- Udyam + DPIIT eligibility
- DPDP notice/consent/breach calendar (Rules 2025, full obligations May 2027)
- Trademark Class search guidance (IP India)

Do **not** auto-file government forms without a licensed professional in the loop. Position as guided preparation + export for the CA.

### 4. Operations that consume the incubated model

After launch, agents should not be a separate “AI employee” product. They should execute **this** ICP, **this** offer, **this** pricing, **this** compliance calendar.

LivePlan Premium’s QuickBooks sync is the only mainstream “plan vs actual” bridge. It still does not know the interview log or the DPDP notice.

## Positioning statement (draft)

> BusinessGenerator is the agentic incubator for founders who will not build until the evidence exists. It turns a description into a company pack you own — strategy, numbers, legal, and operating playbooks — then helps you run the company against that pack. It is not a business-plan writer.

## Wedge sequence (so we do not boil the ocean)

1. **Phase A — Validation studio:** problem, persona, interview coach, alternatives, WTP experiments, GO/PIVOT/KILL. Beat Foundra/LEANSpark on evidence quality and export.
2. **Phase B — Company pack:** canvases, model, PRD, GTM, India legal checklist. Beat LivePlan on consistency and take-away ownership.
3. **Phase C — Ops runtime:** inbox, CRM, invoices, compliance reminders, content, support macros — all bound to the pack. Beat Zoho on “it already knows the business,” not on ERP breadth.

## Risks if we ignore the gap

- Ship another PrometAI and compete on PDF beauty.
- Ship another CoFounderBot and skip interviews; we will help people fail faster.
- Ship India legal as a chatbot and get someone fined (GST, DPDP). Legal agents must cite current law and hand off to humans.
