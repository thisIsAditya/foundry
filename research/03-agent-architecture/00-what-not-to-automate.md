# What not to automate

Automating the wrong thing is how we become CoFounderBot: fast at code, empty at truth.

## Must stay human

| Activity | Why |
|---|---|
| Customer interviews | Trust, body language, intros; Mom Test is a human skill. Agent = coach. |
| Founder sales of the first 10 | Blank: founders sell in Validation. |
| MCA / GST / trademark **submission** | Licensed professional; credential risk; changing portals. |
| Ethics of the idea (Cagan “should we”) | Model will rationalize. |
| Kill vs stubborn vision | Agent can recommend; founder owns the company. |
| Signing contracts, issuing equity | Legal capacity. |
| Handling children’s data / health / money transmission | Regulated. |

## Automate only with approve-first

- Emails and WhatsApp broadcasts  
- Publishing pages  
- Charging cards / creating payment links in bulk  
- Scraping (ToS, law)  
- Changing prices on a live site  

## Never claim to automate

- “500 AI users validated this” as Gate A  
- Auto-pivot that deletes the founder’s beachhead without a review  
- DCF valuation of a pre-revenue idea as a fact  
- “We filed your company”  
- Unsupervised customer impersonation (“the agent hopped on sales calls as you”)

## Soft automation (good)

- Transcripts, quote extraction, scheduling  
- Search with citations  
- Spreadsheet math  
- Drafting PRDs, notices, checklists  
- Reminding GST due dates  
- Generating canvases from the graph  
- Builder prompts  

If a competitor automates a must-stay-human step, **do not copy them.** That is our differentiation.
