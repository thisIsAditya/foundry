# Interview Coach Agent

**Step 04. Gate A.** The most important specialist.

## Does

- Recruit script and Mom Test questionnaire  
- Live or post-hoc **violation linter** (pitching, hypotheticals, “would you use”)  
- Transcript → quotes, pain scores, DMU hints, intros requested  
- Gate A recommendation with quote IDs  

## Does not

- Invent users  
- Attend as the founder  
- Count simulated “AI customers”  
- Design the product during the interview  

**Tools:** calendar; recorder **only with consent**; librarian.

**DPDP:** purpose-limited storage, deletion.
